## Prediction (2026-10-25, before implementation)
Twenty seeds (1–20), 80x24, centre-following trot to row 1800: I predict
35% of spawned shadows perceive a wolf before, 65% after. Centre following
will be tick-scripted from the seeded path, not a stationary screen column.
The shoulder run uses the same controller with a half-width offset.
I predict a fixed parry ten ticks before landing succeeds 0/20 before,
20/20 after a twelve-tick window (six crouch, six lunge). Offset six should
succeed on both; the extra window is proposed, not a ruling.
Cooldown rhythm is 30/20 = 1.50 versus 30/14 = 2.14 leaps/s while stamina
lasts, not sustained indefinitely. The alternating groups are slots 0,1,2
then 3,4; alpha second. A shared triangular integer arc animates both.
Dusk onset at row 600: ideal flat trot 128.0 s before, 111.3 s after;
full dusk 256.0 to 222.6 s, night 384.0 to 333.9 s. Fifteen percent more
speed means 13.04% less elapsed time, not literally fifteen percent less.
Goldens predicted to move: trot, steer-forest, sprint-exhaust, formation-turn,
faint-return, clearing, dense-stand, resize, wide, spawn-chase, group-attack,
leap-lands, parry-turns, death-card, deer-flee, dusk-early, dusk, night;
standstill should hold unless braking distance moves its first frames.
Art sheets move for the leap poses; title sheets should hold.

## Parry, leap cadence and dusk
Fixed landing at tick 12, seeds 1–20, one shadow three cells ahead of
a stationary alpha (the other four are fixture-fainted). Press offsets
6 and 8 ticks: before 20/20 and after 20/20. Offset 10: before 0/20,
after 20/20. Offset 12: 0/20 on both: the end is exclusive, as before.
This measures input timing, not a human's ability to read the new pose.
An initial flank-edge fixture whiffed on five seeds; it was replaced
by the three-row fixture so a whiff could not masquerade as a parry miss.

Leap burst cadence: 1.50/s (20-tick cooldown) -> 2.14/s (14 ticks).
Stamina cost remains 60, so this is a burst, not an indefinitely sustained
rate. Only accepted presses alternate. The alpha is second and never
moves the camera. Shared `leap_arc` is out-and-back and half-cell/tick;
collision clipping can shorten a travel. The shadow gathers backwards
1.5 cells before the six-tick forward beat, opening room even on a flank.
R-pk04-3 remains recommended: animation offsets, unchanged hit envelopes;
bites resolve at landing and vanquishes at the accepted press as before.

Flat full-health trot: 4.6875 -> 5.390625 cells/s. Stop/start tick counts
remain 5/10 using integer BRAKE 10 and ACCEL 5; leaving 8/4 would take
6/12 ticks. Deer walk/fresh/tired 16/60/32 -> 18/69/37. Shadow speeds and
all deer wear-down/perception/recovery timers are unchanged. Dusk onset
128.0 -> 111.3 s; full dusk 256.0 -> 222.6; night 384.0 -> 333.9. These
are ideal flat-trot times; steering, trees, health and stops change them.
Fifteen percent more speed is 13.04% less time, not 15%.
