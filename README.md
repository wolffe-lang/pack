# pack

Lead a pack of wolves up an endless, meandering forest path. Shadow wolves with red eyes come out of the trees;
leap at them as a pack, parry, or sprint past and be chased. Deer feed the pack. There is no health bar: as the
pack weakens, wolves faint and are left behind, and as it recovers they sprint back to rejoin you.

A terminal game written in wolf, the first of the lair, the wolf games collection. It runs in a terminal on
macOS and Linux today and on PAX's console, where the installer offers it.
